Welcome to St. Monica

Empowering Learners with Excellence, Ethics & Holistic Growth

At St. Monica Memorial School, we believe education goes far beyond textbooks. Our motto, "Beyond Learning", reflects our total commitment to fostering emotional intelligence, physical development, moral values, and academic excellence in early childhood and lower primary years.

Located conveniently at Mwihoko, Tumaini Avenue, 160 Stage, our modern facility offers a secure, spotless, and inspiring environment designed specifically for young learners to discover their talents and excel seamlessly under Kenya's Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC).

Loving Environment

Individual attention and patient care for every child.

CBC Competency Focus

Core literacy, numeracy, creative & practical skills.

Safe & Secure

Gated perimeter, supervised play areas, and child protection standards.

Balanced Meals

Nutritious hot meals and teas served daily in clean dining halls.

School Transport

Safe pick-up & drop-off across Mwihoko, Kahawa Wendani, & surrounding areas.

Co-Curricular

Music, art, sports, play-based activities, and talent shows.

Our Academic Programs

Tailored learning stages focused on early development, social integration, and academic foundation under CBC guidelines.

Ages 1 - 2.5 Yrs

Creche

A home away from home. Focused on nurturing basic motor skills, language immersion, rest, and structured social interactions in a clean environment.

  • Individual Nap & Rest Spaces
  • Sensory Play & Storytelling
  • Dedicated Caregivers
Ages 2.5 - 3.5 Yrs

Playgroup

Interactive play-based foundation. Building communication skills, sharing habits, creative coloring, song, and basic toilet independence.

  • Guided Interactive Play
  • Early Speech Development
  • Outdoor & indoor games
CBC Early Years
Ages 4 - 5 Yrs

PP1 & PP2

Pre-Primary 1 & 2 level focusing on pre-literacy, number concepts, environmental activities, creative art, and confidence building.

  • Phonics & Early Reading
  • Number Recognition
  • Environmental Learning
Ages 6 - 8 Yrs

Grades 1 to 7

Comprehensive CBC Lower Primary. Mastery of core competencies, digital literacy, language skills, mathematics, and religious education.

  • Full CBC Curriculum Alignment
